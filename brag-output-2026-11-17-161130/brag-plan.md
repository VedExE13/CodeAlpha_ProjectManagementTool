# Brag Plan: Workbench

## What is this app?
Workbench is a collaborative project board for creating projects, assigning tasks, and keeping notes beside the work.

## The angle
Show the board as a working surface, not an analytics dashboard: clear columns, one task in motion, and its note attached to its context.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
“Good work has a place to land.” reveals a three-column task board.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Three real statuses: To do, In progress, and Done.
- A task card moves from one column to another.
- Task detail keeps priority, status, and a sample note in one place.

## Outro / punchline
“Keep the next step in view.”

## User flow worth showing
Open a project board → move a task → keep a note with that task.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: A composed project desk: decisive type, mineral surfaces, and a three-column board in motion.
- Interpretation: Direct pacing, quiet motion, and restrained warm signals on pale mineral surfaces.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: pale mineral green `#e8ece5`
- Accent: muted ochre `#d2ac7b`
- Text: forest ink `#1e2824`
- Display font: DM Sans in product; Arial fallback in film.
- Body font: DM Sans in product; Arial fallback in film.
- Strongest visual element: the three status columns and card movement.

## Share copy (draft)
Workbench keeps projects, tasks, and decisions together on a shared board. Move the work forward one card at a time.

## Audio direction
- Role: restrained, warm instrumental bed.
- Music: Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 1, Sascha Ende (CC BY 4.0).
- Music treatment: low in the mix; trim to 20 seconds and trim to 20 seconds beneath the final mark.
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue preset; card move resolves near the 17.02s strong cue. Sequential cards use the half-second grid.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none; extraction support unavailable in this local setup.
- SFX posture: sparse; a quiet paper-like click only on the task move.
- Audio-coupled moments: board reveal; task card move; final wordmark.
- Restraint rule: no invented metrics, velocity claims, or calculated analytics.

## Storyboard
### Scene 1 — The board — 3s
The actual three work states form the visual hook. Sequential/interaction: board columns unfold after the line. Audio intent: calm, purposeful. Audio-coupled idea: columns arrive on a soft beat.
### Scene 2 — A project in view — 5s
Use fictional task titles to illustrate the actual board structure. Sequential/interaction: three columns and cards arrive one by one. Audio intent: a steady pulse. Audio-coupled idea: stagger each column to the beat grid.
### Scene 3 — Move a task — 4.5s
A card travels from To do to In progress. Sequential/interaction: yes, one visible drag. Audio intent: one quiet contact accent. Audio-coupled idea: the card lands near a strong cue.
### Scene 4 — Keep context — 4.5s
Task detail includes priority, status, and an obviously fictional sample note. Sequential/interaction: note unfolds beneath the task. Audio intent: settle. Audio-coupled idea: copy fades in naturally.
### Scene 5 — Close — 3s
Workbench name and “Keep the next step in view.” Sequential/interaction: none. Intentional reading hold is annotated in the composition. Audio intent: low, warm finish. Audio-coupled idea: music fades under the final line.
